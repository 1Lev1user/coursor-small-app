## Goal
The Add expense form gets the same "Refund (money back from a shop)" checkbox that the Month editor has, so a refund can be recorded when it happens instead of being saved as a normal expense and corrected later.

## Acceptance
- `node --test test/addRefund.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): Add expense with the Refund checkbox unchecked and checked (the Save-as-template row hidden when checked), and the "Refund added" confirmation, at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; checkbox row at least 44 px high; owner checks
- Manual (owner): add a EUR 12.99 expense with Refund checked; Month shows it as a refund (minus sign, "Refund" tag) and Spent drops by 12.99; the confirmation says "Refund added"

## Do not touch
- SCHEMA_VERSION and MIGRATIONS (the `refund` field already exists on expenses)
- `spendCents` and the totals in src/budget.js
- The Month editor (src/views/month.js:583-593) and the bank-import refund handling
- existing test files
- Limit for `src/views/add.js`: the `draft` object, `renderExpenseForm`, `addScreenTitle` and `renderAddedConfirm` for the title, and one new exported function.
