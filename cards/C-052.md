## Goal
While the current month is not over, "Changes vs last month" in Chart > Trends and the comparison line in Month compare spending so far with the same days of last month (and say so), instead of a 4-day October against a full September. When last month has no spending at all, no "up" or "down" is claimed.

- `node --test test/analytics.test.js` exits 0

- Manual (owner): on 4 October with spending in September, Month no longer says "EUR365.03 less than September 2026" for the first four days; it says how it compares with the first four days of September; opening September itself still compares the whole month with August

`src/analytics.js`:
- `export function partialDay(monthKey, now = new Date())`: the day of the month of `now` when `monthKey === currentMonthKey(now)` and that day is less than `daysInMonth(monthKey)`; otherwise `null` (a finished month, a future month, or the last day of the month is compared in full). Import `daysInMonth` from `./months.js`.
- `export function dataUpToDay(data, lastDay)`: a shallow copy `{ ...data, expenses: data.expenses.filter(...) }` keeping expenses whose day of month (`Number(date.slice(8, 10))`) is at most `lastDay`. Only expenses are filtered; callers use it for spending only.
- `categoryChanges(data, monthKey, options = {})` (:74): when `options.lastDay` is a number, run the whole function on `dataUpToDay(data, options.lastDay)` (current month, previous month and the six-month average all cut at the same day). Without the option the behaviour is unchanged.
`src/views/trends.js` `renderChanges` (:306-345): `const lastDay = partialDay(ctx.monthKey, new Date())`; pass `{ lastDay }` to `categoryChanges`. Sentence (exact): finished month, unchanged `September 2026 compared with August 2026. Average covers up to 6 earlier months with spending.`; partial month `October 2026 so far (1-4 October) compared with the same days of September 2026. Average covers up to 6 earlier months, same days.` When no expense in the previous month falls inside the compared days (`categoryChanges` returns `previousCents === 0` for every row), the sentence becomes `No spending recorded in September 2026 to compare with.` and `changeText` (:279-287) is replaced in the list by `nothing to compare` for every row (pass a flag into `renderChangeItems`, :289-306).
`src/views/month.js`: in `render` (:949-968) compute `lastDay = partialDay(ctx.monthKey, new Date())` and, when set, `previousTotals = monthTotals(dataUpToDay(ctx.data, lastDay), previousMonthKey)`; `comparisonText` (:150-163) gets the extra argument and reads `EUR X more than the same days of September 2026` / `... less than ...` / `Same as the same days of September 2026` when partial; the empty-previous case keeps `No spending recorded in September 2026`.
