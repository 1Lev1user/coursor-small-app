## Goal
Until a month has ended, the comparison with the previous month is hidden (owner decision 19): the Month header line "EUR X more/less than <previous month>" is not shown, and Chart > Trends does not show the "Changes vs last month" card at all. A 4-day October is never set against a full September. For a finished month whose previous month has no spending, no "up" or "down" is claimed.

## Acceptance
- `node --test test/monthFinished.test.js` exits 0
- `node --test test/months.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): Trends in the current month (no "Changes vs last month" card), in a finished month with a list, and in a finished month whose previous month is empty; and the Month header in the current month (no comparison line) and in a finished month (line shown), at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; the sentences wrap without overflow; owner checks
- Manual (owner): on 4 October with spending in September, Month no longer says "EUR365.03 less than September 2026" and Trends does not list changes; opening September itself still compares the whole month with August; after the month turns over, October shows the comparison
