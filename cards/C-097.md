## Goal
The camt reader takes `PrtryAmt` as a possible original-currency amount (src/import/xml.js `camtCounterAmounts`, ~421-434). In the only open Baltic camt sample (DNB Bankas LT), every entry repeats the same EUR value three times: InstdAmt, TxAmt and PrtryAmt with Tp EQUIVALENT. A Swiss sample has PrtryAmt Tp IBS 0.03 next to an entry of 4500.00. PrtryAmt is bank-specific and informational (research/3.1/bank-shapes.md). After this card PrtryAmt is never used as an amount, and only InstdAmt, TxAmt or CntrValAmt in a currency different from the entry's count as the original amount.
