## Goal
Settings opens as eight collapsed rows, each with a one-line summary, in a useful order (backup within the first three rows); tapping a row opens that group, one at a time. This replaces the 9-screen scroll and the static jump chips.

## Acceptance
- `node --test test/settingsGroups.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): Settings with all rows collapsed (the whole list fits one phone screen), one group open, and the Backup row in its warning state, at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; row height at least 44 px; chevron and summary readable; owner checks
- Manual (owner): tap each row; only one group is open at a time; Home > month review "Review categories" still lands on the Categories group; after saving inside an open group it stays open; keyboard: Tab to a row, Enter or Space opens it, focus stays on the row

## Do not touch
- SCHEMA_VERSION and MIGRATIONS
- The section modules' own content (plan.js, income.js, subscriptions.js, goals.js, backup.js, importSettings.js, rights.js)
- Section ids `more-plan`, `more-income`, `more-subscriptions`, `more-categories`, `more-goals`, `more-import`, `more-rules`, `more-layouts`, `more-backup`, `more-rights` (add.js:341 and 387 deep-link to them through `openSettingsSection`)
- existing test files
- Limit for `src/views/settings/categories.js`: add `export` to `userCategories` (:50) only.
- Limit for `style.css`: new `.settings-*` rules with tokens only; remove the now unused `.more-jumps`/`.more-jump` rules (:969-985) and the `.more-jump` entry in the reduced-motion list (:897).
