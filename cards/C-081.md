## Goal
At the 3.0 update, and for every new owner after the normal Setup, the app shows the one-time money setup before Home: how much is on the card now, and the paydays of the regular incomes. Finishing it starts the new counting; it cannot be skipped (decisions 9 and 34; part B of the split C-034).

## Acceptance
- `grep -c "balanceStart" src/app.js` prints 1 or more
- `grep -c "renderMoneySetup" src/app.js` prints 2
- `node --test test/moneySetup.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): the money setup (empty, with a prefilled Salary row, with an error on the amount and on a day, three rows) at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; owner checks
- Manual (owner): data from 2.0 with usual income 2,000: after the update the setup appears before Home, with a Salary row of 2,000.00 and an empty payday; entering 1,250.50 and day 25 and tapping Start opens Home; no earlier month changed in Month
- Manual (owner): fresh profile: Setup (name, budget, savings), then the money setup, then Home

## Do not touch
- `applyMoneySetup` (C-034) except to call it
- src/budget.js, src/incomeSources.js, src/balance.js, src/model.js, SCHEMA_VERSION and MIGRATIONS
- style.css (reuse `setup-page`, `card stack setup-form`, `field`, `btn btn-primary`, `btn btn-ghost`, `muted`)
- existing test files
- Limit for `src/app.js`: one import and one gate block in `render()`.
