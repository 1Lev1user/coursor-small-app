## Goal
A transfer from the card to Revolut or to a savings account takes money off the card, but the import does not store transfer rows at all (buildImport keeps them only for duplicate checks), so Money now stays too high (owner, 2026-10-18). After this card an imported transfer is stored as an entry that changes Money now and the Month opening and closing money, and that never counts as spending or income: not in the budget, Home spending, the per-day spending of today, charts, income totals, savings goals or search totals.

## Notes
- Planner decides the data shape and states the consequences; prefer the option that needs no SCHEMA_VERSION bump:
  - (a) Transfer entries as expenses/incomes in a new system category "Transfers" (pattern: Savings / Uncategorised system categories in model.js, `spendCents`, budget exclusions). Check every place that sums expenses or incomes (budget.js, balance.js `perDay` today's spending, charts, month totals, search totals, income sources, goals) and list which ones must skip the category.
  - (b) A new `transfers` list: needs SCHEMA_VERSION 4, a migration step and a test; 3.1 could not read the data afterwards (rollback note in the release).
- Only imported transfers are in scope (kind 'transfer' in the import). Manual entry of a transfer is out of scope.
- Undo of an import removes its transfer entries too (C-105 edited-entry logic applies to them like to other entries).
- Money now must equal: start + incomes − expenses + transfers in − transfers out, dated from the setup date.
- Tests: an out and an in transfer change `balanceAt`, `moneyNow` and `monthBalance`; budget spending, Home spending, per-day spent-today, charts data, income totals and search totals are unchanged by them; re-import marks them as duplicates; undo removes them; a 3.1.0 backup without transfers loads unchanged.
