## Goal
Some banks and Revolut-style exports write dates with a month name ("17 Sep 2026", "17-Sep-26", "Sep 17, 2026", "17. septembris 2026", "17 сентября 2026"). Today every such row is "Date not recognised" (research/3.0/D-bank.md section 16, VERIFIED). Rows dated far in the future or decades ago (13.09.2062, 14.09.1926) are imported silently. After this card, month-name dates in English, Latvian and Russian (full and short forms, any case) are read. The check panel lists how many rows are dated more than 1 year in the future or more than 15 years in the past, and those rows start unticked.

## Notes
- Also (research/3.1/bank-shapes.md, READ): Revolut writes `YYYY-MM-DD H:MM:SS` with a one-digit hour (`2021-09-06 6:11:59`); the ISO branch of `parseDateWith` (text.js ~683) requires two digits and must accept one. Wise `Date Time` is `DD-MM-YYYY HH:MM:SS.mmm`: the date part must parse with DMY. Add both to the tests.
- `parseDateWith` (text.js ~677) and `inferDateFormat` know DMY/MDY/YMD with digits only. Add one more format, 'TEXT'. It parses a day, a month name and a year in any order the examples above use, with 2-digit years as 20xx. `inferDateFormat` returns 'TEXT' when every non-empty sample parses that way.
- Month names:
  - English: Jan…Dec, full and 3-letter.
  - Latvian: janvāris…decembris, plus the short forms jan, febr, marts, apr, maijs, jūn, jūl, aug, sept, okt, nov, dec, with or without diacritics.
  - Russian: январь…декабрь, plus the genitive forms января…декабря and short forms янв…дек.
- The date question in the Columns step (import.js) gets the option "Day and month name (17 Sep 2026)".
- Sanity range: rows dated after `now` + 365 days or before `now` − 15 years are counted in the check panel and pre-unticked. `now` is passed in; tests use a fixed now.
- Tests: each language and form; '17-Sep-26'; 'Sep 17, 2026'; a column mixing digits and names gives null; the far-future and far-past counts with a fixed now.
