## Goal
A saved rule sorts every row whose text matches, whatever the amount. If NARVESEN is usually 2-10 € and a 50 € payment arrives, it disappears into the Known group. After this card such a row keeps the rule's category but is shown in New with the note "Unusual amount for this shop (usually €2-€10)", so you see it before importing.

## Notes
- Usual range of a rule: the amounts of stored entries the rule matches (same matcher), in EUR cents. Use only when there are at least 5 such entries; otherwise no guard.
- Unusual: above the 3rd quartile + 3 × IQR, or below the 1st quartile − 3 × IQR (floored at 0), with a minimum IQR of 1.00 € so that a shop with always the same price (a subscription) is not flagged by a few cents. The planner may tune the factor; tests pin it.
- The row's decision keeps the rule's category and ruleId; only its placement (New instead of Known) and the note change. Shown range: the 10th to 90th percentile, rounded to whole euros.
- Tests: fewer than 5 entries (no guard); 2-10 € history and 50 € row (flagged); 9.99 € (not flagged); same-price subscription and a 1-cent change (not flagged); income rules; refunds ignored in the history.
- Amendment (card-planner at 92be7da, 2026-10-18):
  - Decision key `amountGuard: { kind: 'expense'|'income', lowCents, highCents }` only when a row is flagged (existing tests deepEqual whole decisions; never an undefined key). The row keeps the rule's kind, category, ruleId and showAs.
  - Guarded: rules of kind expense (money out, non-refund) and income, with an integer amount above 0. Not refund, transfer or skip rules, and not the money-in-matches-a-shop-rule refund fallback.
  - History: the stored entries the rule wins under the C-099 matching (whole word, direction fit, longest pattern, then list order), with an import id, text `bankText || note`. Expense rules count non-refund expenses, income rules count incomes. Built at most once per `defaultDecisions` call.
  - Statistics: ascending sort; quartiles by linear interpolation at h = p*(n-1) (Excel QUARTILE.INC); at least 5 entries; used IQR = max(Q3 - Q1, 100 cents). Flagged when amount > Q3 + 3*IQR or amount < max(0, Q1 - 3*IQR). The shown range is the 10th to 90th percentile in whole euros ("usually €N" when equal).
  - Text: "Unusual amount for this shop (usually €2-€10)"; for an income rule "Unusual amount for this income source (usually €2000-€2500)".
  - Limit for `src/views/import.js`: pure exported `amountGuardNote(decision)` and `isKnownDecision(decision)` (ruleId set and no amountGuard), used for the Known/New split and its error jump, and one line in renderDecisionRow. Reuse existing classes; no CSS change.
