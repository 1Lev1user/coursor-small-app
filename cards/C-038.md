## Notes
Design, all of it derived rather than stored (stored closing balances would drift with every edit, Undo, import undo and restore): only the anchor `settings.balanceStart = { cents, date }` is saved; every other figure is computed from the entries. The anchor means "the money I had at the start of that date", so entries dated on the anchor date count. Months before the anchor month have no balance.

What counts: real income entries and expenses with `date >= anchor.date`. Expected income that has not been confirmed never counts (it is not money yet). Refunds are expenses with `refund: true`; `spendCents` (budget.js:19) gives them a negative spend, so they add to the balance. Bank-import transfers are not entries, so they are ignored. Entries dated after the day asked about are ignored (a Netflix row dated next week does not lower money now). Savings: `export const SAVINGS_LEAVES_BALANCE = true;` money booked in the Savings category (goal contributions included) leaves the balance, because it is set aside and not spendable; if the owner decides otherwise (decision in _index) change the one constant. `balanceAt(data, dateISO, options = {})` takes `options.savingsLeavesBalance` for tests.

Exports of src/balance.js (C-039 imports these names):
- `parseBalanceInput(text)`: like `parseAmount` (money.js:14) but accepts a leading '-' and zero, returns integer cents or null.
- `setBalanceStart(data, cents, dateISO)`: `{ ok: false, reason }` when cents is not a safe integer or `monthKeyOf(dateISO)` is null ('Enter a valid date.'); otherwise sets `data.settings.balanceStart` and returns `{ ok: true }`.
- `balanceAt(data, dateISO, options)`: null when there is no anchor or `dateISO < anchor.date`; otherwise `anchor.cents` plus incomes minus `spendCents` of expenses with `anchor.date <= date <= dateISO` (end of that day).
- `moneyNow(data, now)` = `balanceAt(data, todayISO(now))`.
- `monthBalance(data, monthKey)`: null when no anchor or the month is before the anchor's month; else `{ openingCents, closingCents, startsOn }`. In the anchor's own month `openingCents = anchor.cents` and `startsOn = anchor.date`; in later months `openingCents = balanceAt(data, last day of the previous month)` and `startsOn` is the first day of the month. `closingCents = balanceAt(data, last day of monthKey)`. A month's opening always equals the previous month's closing.
- `correctBalance(data, targetCents, now)`: "my bank says X now". Needs an anchor and a safe integer target; sets `anchor.cents += targetCents - moneyNow(data, now)` and returns `{ ok: true, shiftCents }`. This rewrites every opening and closing figure back to the anchor (the screen in C-039 must say so). `{ ok: false, reason: 'Set your starting balance first.' }` without an anchor.
- `nextPayday(data, now)`: the earliest payday date strictly after today among active income sources whose month state (from `incomeStatus`, C-030) is 'upcoming', looking at the current and the next month; `{ date, sourceId, name }` or null. A payday that is today or earlier and not yet confirmed is ignored (money may be arriving; it is not a horizon).
- `safeToSpend(data, now)`: null without an anchor. Otherwise: `moneyCents = moneyNow`; horizon = `nextPayday(...).date`, or when none, the first day of next month (`mode: 'monthEnd'`, else `'payday'`); `days = whole days from today to the horizon`, at least 1; `billsCents` = sum of `amountCents` of every subscription whose due date `dateInMonth(monthKey, dayOfMonth)` (current month, and the next month when the horizon is in it) is before the horizon and which is not paid and not skipped for that month (use `isPaidThisMonth` and `skippedMonths` from C-035; a reminder still open for a day that already passed counts); `availableCents = moneyCents - billsCents`; `perDayCents = Math.floor(availableCents / days)` (may be negative: the screen shows "not enough"). Returns `{ perDayCents, days, untilDate, mode, paydayName, moneyCents, billsCents, availableCents }`.

Worked numbers for tests (2026 calendar; 15 Oct 2026 is a Thursday, 25 Oct 2026 a Sunday): anchor `{ cents: 100000, date: '2026-10-01' }`; income 150000 on 2026-10-05; expense 25000 on 2026-10-10; now `new Date(2026, 9, 15)`:
- `moneyNow` = 225000. A 30000 expense dated 2026-10-20 does not change it. A 4000 refund (spend -4000) on 2026-10-12 raises it to 229000.
- An entry dated 2026-09-30 is ignored. `balanceAt(data, '2026-09-30')` is null.
- Source Salary 200000 day 25 weekend 'keep', incomeSourcesFrom '2026-10', not received: `nextPayday` is 2026-10-25; subscription Netflix 1500 day 20 unpaid: `safeToSpend` gives `days` 10, `billsCents` 1500, `availableCents` 223500, `perDayCents` 22350, mode 'payday'.
- Same data without any source: horizon 2026-11-01, `days` 17, `perDayCents` 13147, mode 'monthEnd'.
- Today 25 Oct 2026 with the salary still unconfirmed: horizon is the next month's payday 2026-11-25, `days` 31.
- Salary already received this month: `nextPayday` is the November date.
- Subscription paid by a bank row (C-035 matching) or skipped does not count in `billsCents`.
- `monthBalance(data, '2026-10')`: opening 100000, startsOn '2026-10-01'; closing includes entries to 31 Oct, including ones dated after `now`; `monthBalance(data, '2026-11').openingCents` equals that closing; September is null.
- `correctBalance(data, 230000, now)` after the 225000 case returns `shiftCents` 5000 and `moneyNow` is then 230000.
- Savings: a 10000 expense in category savings lowers `moneyNow` by 10000 by default and not with `{ savingsLeavesBalance: false }`.
- `setBalanceStart` refuses a non-integer and an invalid date; `parseBalanceInput('-12,50')` is -1250, '0' is 0, 'abc' is null.
