## Notes
Found while planning C-014 (2026-10-05): README.md still describes 2.0 ("Usual monthly income", "Settings > Plan", "Settings > Import", "Settings > Backup & export", "Version 2.0 changed the data format"). Rewrite only the sections "First-run setup", "Everyday use", "Importing a bank statement", "Backup, import, and CSV" and "Updates"; keep "Install on phone". Use the app's real strings (check with `git grep` in src/):
- First run: "Your name", "Monthly budget (EUR)", Savings in € or %; then "Set up your money": the amount on the card now and the regular incomes ("Name", "Expected amount (EUR)", "Payday (day of month)"); earlier months stay as they were. Existing users see that screen once after the update.
- Home: "Money now" (every expense lowers it, every income raises it), the per-day line (Settings group "Money" > "Amount per day": "Automatic" or "Fixed amount"), the month budget line; tap Money now to check against the bank: a difference is added as one dated entry with the note "Bank difference", edited or deleted in Month.
- Paydays: Settings group "Income" > "Regular income"; on the payday Home asks for the amount received ("Received", "Later", "Skip this month"); nothing is added without a tap.
- Add expense: currency, "Refund (money back from a shop)", "Save as template"; Quick add on Home with Undo; Settings group "Quick add" renames, re-prices or deletes templates.
- Month: starts/ends line, the regular-income lines, comparison with last month only once the month is over.
- Settings is one page of groups, one open at a time: Money, Income, Monthly budget, Backup, Categories and limits, Subscriptions, Quick add, Goals, Bank import (advanced), Profile and about.
- Import: Settings group "Bank import (advanced)" > "Import a bank statement"; "Check what was read" totals; "Reverse money in and out"; possible duplicates are not imported unless ticked; a payday or subscription row may ask "Is this the …?". Keep the existing paragraphs on formats, rules, undo and FiDAViSTA, with the paths updated.
- Backup: Settings group "Backup": "Export backup (JSON)"; "Import backup" shows what the file holds and offers "Settings only" or "Replace everything" (your current data is downloaded first); "Data from before the last update" with download and restore; month CSV in Europe or Standard format.
- Updates: the Update bar; version 3.0 converts the data once and keeps the old copy under Settings > Backup.
Plain language, short; no screenshots in the README.
