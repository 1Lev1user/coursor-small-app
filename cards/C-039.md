## Goal
Home's main number is Money now; tapping it opens "How much is on the card now?" and adds one dated difference. Below it come the per-day amount (fixed or automatic) and the month budget. Settings > Money sets the per-day mode and lists the bank checks. Month shows what the month started and ended with and, for each regular income, what was expected against what arrived.

## Notes
Owner decisions (research/3.0/decisions-3.0.md): 6 Home main number: Money now, then the per-day amount, then the month budget; 7 per-day amount in two modes chosen by the user (fixed, showing what is left of today's amount; automatic); 8 tapping Money now opens "How much is on the card now?", the app adds one dated difference entry, past months unchanged; 1 savings leave Money now and are shown as a separate amount; 14 the Settings group order has Money first.

Imports from src/balance.js (C-038): `moneyNow, perDay, savedCents, checkAgainstBank, deleteBankCheck, setPerDay, monthBalance, parseBalanceInput`; from src/incomeSources.js (C-030): `incomeStatus`; months.js: `todayISO, fullDate, shortDate`; money.js: `formatEuro, formatPlain`. src/views/homeMoney.js (new; no `document` access at module top level):
- `export function signedEuro(cents)`: '+' prefix for positive amounts, `formatEuro` otherwise.
- `export function perDayText(data, now)`: null without an anchor. Auto: `${formatEuro(amountCents)} a day for ${days} days until ${untilText}` (`1 day` when 1) where `untilText` is `payday ${shortDate(untilDate)}` for `untilKind` 'payday' and 'the end of the month' for 'monthEnd'; when `amountCents < 0` it reads `Nothing left per day until ${untilText}`. Fixed: `${formatEuro(leftTodayCents)} left of today's ${formatEuro(amountCents)}`, and when over `${formatEuro(-leftTodayCents)} over today's ${formatEuro(amountCents)}`.
- `export function savedText(data, now)`: null unless `savedCents` is above 0; `Put into Savings since ${shortDate(anchor.date)}: ${formatEuro(saved)}`.
- `export function checkPreview(data, text, now)`: `{ ok: false, reason }` ('Set up Money now first.' without an anchor, 'Enter an amount such as 1250.00 or -40.00.' when `parseBalanceInput` is null), else `{ ok: true, enteredCents, moneyCents, differenceCents, text }` where `text` is 'Matches the app. Nothing will be added.' for 0 and otherwise `The app shows ${formatEuro(moneyCents)}. Save: `parseBalanceInput`, `checkAgainstBank`; on `ok` then `ctx.save()`; when it returns false remove the pushed adjustment again (`data.balanceAdjustments.pop()` when one was added) and keep the sheet; toast 'Matches your bank. Nothing added.' for 0 or `Difference added: ${signedEuro(diff)}`.
