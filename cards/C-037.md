## Goal
A past month imported with "Only record spending (no budget)" shows what was spent without calling it over budget, and money added to Savings or a goal does not turn Home and Month red when Savings has no budget share.

- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): Month for a "no budget" past month; Month and Home after adding EUR150 to a goal with no Savings share; at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; owner checks

## Notes
Seen in the running app:
1. A past month imported with "Only record spending (no budget)" (import/core.js:569-580 stores budget 0, every limit 0, `actualOnly: true`) shows "Budget left -EUR45.10 over budget", "Spent EUR45.10 of EUR0.00" and "Necessary EUR45.10 of no budget - over by EUR45.10" in red.
2. Adding EUR150 to a goal (goals.js:187-219 books an expense in Savings) when Savings has limit 0: Month shows "Savings EUR150.00 of no budget - over by EUR150.00" and Home flips to "over budget".

budget.js `monthTotals` (:200-252, `categoryTotal` :254-269), after C-031:
- Add to the result: `hasBudget` (`plan.actualOnly !== true`), `budgetSpentCents`, `outsideBudgetCents`, and compute `budgetLeftCents = budgetCents - budgetSpentCents`.
- No budget: when `plan.actualOnly === true`, build each category row from `{ ...entry, noLimit: true }` so `over` is false and `overByCents` 0. `budgetLeftCents` keeps its number; views must read `hasBudget`.
- Savings without a share: let `savingsEntry = plan.entries.find(({ id }) => id === SAVINGS_ID)` (import `SAVINGS_ID` from './model.js'). When it exists and `savingsEntry.limitCents === 0`, its row is built with `noLimit: true` (never over), and its spending is left out of the budget: `outsideBudgetCents` = that category's `spentCents`, `budgetSpentCents = spentCents - outsideBudgetCents`. When Savings has a share (limit above 0) nothing changes: it counts against the budget and can be over its own limit, as the tests at test/budget.test.js:407-453 expect. Otherwise `outsideBudgetCents` is 0 and `budgetSpentCents` equals `spentCents`.
- `spentCents` (all spending, used for Cash left, the balance, charts, exports) is unchanged.

monthReview.js: after `const totals = monthTotals(...)` (:22) add `if (!totals.hasBudget) return null;` so a "no budget" month never produces a review card. The review card text at add.js:310 ('over spend budget by') is then only reached with a budget.

month.js:
- `renderSummary` (:127-148): when `totals.hasBudget` is false, show only the 'Cash left' headline, and the line `Spent ${formatEuro(totals.spentCents)} · Income ${formatEuro(totals.incomeCents)}` plus a muted line 'No budget for this month.' Otherwise unchanged. (If C-039 landed first, keep its added lines.)
- `renderCategories` (:165-241): the call at :964 passes `totals.budgetCents`; when a `noLimit` row exists and `budgetCents === 0` show only `formatEuro(category.spentCents)` (no '0% of budget', no 'No limit' pill). For the Savings row with `noLimit` and a budget above 0 show the pill text 'Saved, not part of the spend budget' instead of 'No limit'.

add.js `renderHome` (:633-652): export `homeFigureModel(totals)` returning `{ over, label, valueCents, sub }`: `over = totals.budgetLeftCents < 0`; `label` 'over budget by' or 'left to spend'; `valueCents = Math.abs(totals.budgetLeftCents)`; `sub = \`${formatEuro(totals.budgetSpentCents)} spent of ${formatEuro(totals.budgetCents)}\`` plus ` · ${formatEuro(totals.outsideBudgetCents)} saved` when `outsideBudgetCents > 0`. Use it in `renderHome`. Home only shows the current month, which always has a budget plan; do not change what happens when the budget is 0.

test/budgetDisplay.test.js (new, fixed `now`): (1) a month with `monthPlans['2026-04']` built from `buildPlanSnapshot` plus the actual-only changes (`monthlyBudgetCents: 0`, limits 0, `actualOnly: true`) and 4510 spent: `hasBudget` false, every category `over` false, `overByCents` 0; (2) Savings with no share, budget 100000, 15000 in Savings and 20000 in necessary: `budgetSpentCents` 20000, `outsideBudgetCents` 15000, `budgetLeftCents` 80000, `spentCents` 35000, savings row `over` false and `noLimit` true; (3) Savings with a share of 10000 and 15000 spent: counted in `budgetSpentCents`, row `over` true, `overByCents` 5000; (4) `homeFigureModel` for (2) gives label 'left to spend', value 80000, sub containing 'saved'; (5) `getMonthReviewSuggestion` returns null for a previous month with an actual-only plan and spending.

Unverified: no test currently spends in Savings with limit 0 through `monthTotals` (grep of test/budget.test.js found none), so the new `noLimit` value for that row should not break a deep-equal; run `npm test` first.
