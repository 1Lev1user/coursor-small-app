## Goal
camt.053 and camt.052 entries that the bank marks as reversals (`RvslInd` true) are no longer imported as ordinary new expenses or income. They are left out and the import warns how many were left out, so the person can check them. Today the flag is never read and a reversal is counted as another payment (original 25.00 plus a reversal 25.00 gives money out 50.00).

## Notes
Source: D-bank.md problem 10 (reversals; VERIFIED behaviour, real-bank semantics UNVERIFIED; probes p07_camt.mjs C2, p11_camt2.mjs C2b), independently confirmed in D-bank-verify.md item 7 (probe c7.mjs; `grep -ri "RvslInd\|reversal" src/` returns nothing). How banks set `CdtDbtInd` on a reversal entry differs between guidelines (some keep the original direction) and could not be checked offline: verify with the owner's sample file. This is why the card skips and warns instead of flipping the direction.

Cause: `camtEntry` (src/import/xml.js:472-527) reads status, `CdtDbtInd`, date and amount but never `RvslInd`.

Fix: in `camtEntry`, after the status check (:473-476), read `RvslInd` (text, case-insensitive; 'true' or '1' means reversal) and return null with `counts.reversal += 1`. Add `reversal: 0` to the counts object in `parseCamt` (:543) and a warning in `skippedWarnings` (:354-369): `Skipped N reversal entries marked by the bank. Check them by hand.` (use `plural`). The original payment stays imported; the warning is the visible signal. The warning array is shown by the review step already (acceptLoaded passes `result.warnings`).
