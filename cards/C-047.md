## Goal
One tap on a Home quick-add button still adds the expense at once, but the toast now offers Undo, and a second tap on the same button within 1.5 seconds does not add a second entry.

## Acceptance
- `node --test test/quickAddGuard.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): Home with the "Added Coffee" toast and its Undo button at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; Undo target at least 24x24 CSS px (the toast button already uses `--tap`); owner checks
- Manual (owner): tap Coffee once, then Undo within 8 seconds: the entry is gone and the totals are back; double-tap Coffee quickly: one entry only; tap again after two seconds: a second entry is added

## Do not touch
- SCHEMA_VERSION and MIGRATIONS
- `src/views/month.js` and the toast function in src/app.js (the action toast and `UNDO_TOAST_MS` exist since C-023)
- Template data and `templateToExpense`
- existing test files
- Limit for `src/templates.js`: one new pure exported function, `isRepeatTap`, and its constant.
- Limit for `src/views/add.js`: `addFromTemplate` (:580-605), the click handler in `renderTemplates` (:622), and the `model.js` import line.
