## Goal
Since C-094 a token with letters glued to digits can become the offered rule keyword even when it is not a name. Three shapes from the C-094 review (PR #86) are affected:
- a reference or terminal code before the merchant: 'TERM1234 RIMI' gives 'TERM' and 'Nr1234 TELE2' gives 'NR';
- an IBAN in the description: 'LV80HABA0551012345678 ...' gives 'LV80HABA';
- an amount glued to a currency: 'EUR12.50 RIMI' gives 'EUR12.50'.
After this card those rows offer the merchant ('RIMI', 'TELE2'). None of these shapes was seen in the owner's Citadele export; they come from synthetic probes.
