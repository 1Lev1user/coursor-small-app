## Goal
Every button belongs to one family, "tinted blocks" (owner decision 26): solid pine primary, a visible pine-tinted secondary, red only inside a confirm step, small Edit/Delete chips with a full tap area, a quick press response, and in dark mode a pine primary that no longer outshines the hero number (decision 27, dark primary).

## Acceptance
- `node --test test/buttonStyles.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): Home, Add expense, Month (rows with Edit/Delete and an open delete confirm), Settings, Import step 1, at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; one solid primary per screen state; the secondary reads as a button on the sage ground and on a card; owner compares with research/3.0 variant shot `buttons-A`
- Manual (owner): on the iPhone each button visibly darkens and shrinks slightly at the moment of the tap, not after
