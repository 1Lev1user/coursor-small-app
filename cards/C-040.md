## Goal
Settings shows each thing once and uses one word for one meaning: remove the duplicate "Add extra income" form, the double "Settings" heading and the orphan leftover note, and replace the confusing labels listed in Notes.

## Acceptance
- `node --test test/settingsWording.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- Manual (design-review): Settings > Income, Categories and Subscriptions, the Home Add income form and the Chart Income empty state at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; no clipped or overflowing text after the label changes; owner checks
- Manual (owner): Settings shows the word "Settings" once; the Income section has no form, only the list and the income categories; the category buttons read "Limit"; the leftover-split note sits inside Categories

## Do not touch
- SCHEMA_VERSION and MIGRATIONS
- src/budget.js (the name 'Usual salary (Plan)' is asserted by test/budget.test.js:612; the money-model cards own it)
- Sentences that explain how the usual salary is counted (src/views/add.js:726-731, src/views/settings/plan.js:191-196, src/views/setup.js:365, src/views/chartView.js:166 and 174): the money cards rewrite them
- The Home month-review texts that say "spend budget" (src/views/add.js:311-374)
- existing test files
- Limit for `src/views/settings/income.js`: delete the Add form and its draft code; keep the entry list, the entry editor and the income categories.
- Limit for `src/views/add.js`: the four strings listed in Notes only.
- Limit for `README.md`: the one line that names the budget field.
