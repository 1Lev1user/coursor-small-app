## Goal
Part B of the Settings groups (owner decisions 13 and 14): Settings opens as collapsed group rows in the owner's order, each row with its one-line summary from C-041; tapping a row opens that group and closes the one that was open. The jump list goes.

## Acceptance
- `node --test test/settingsGroupsRender.test.js` exits 0
- `node --test test/settingsGroups.test.js` exits 0
- `npm test` exits 0
- `grep -c "more-jump\|add-extra-income-form" style.css` prints 0
- Manual (design-review): Settings with all rows collapsed (the list fits one phone screen), one group open, and the Backup row in its warning state, at 390, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark; row height at least 44 px; chevron and summary readable; owner checks
- Manual (owner): tap each row; only one group is open at a time; Home > month review "Review categories" opens and scrolls to Categories and limits, "Change spending budget" opens Monthly budget; after saving inside an open group it stays open; keyboard: Tab to a row, Enter or Space opens it, focus stays on the row

- `SETTINGS_GROUPS`, `SECTION_RENDERERS`, `groupSummary` and the other exports from C-041 (use them; change them only if a bug blocks this card, and say so)
- The content of the section modules (src/views/settings/*)
- SCHEMA_VERSION and MIGRATIONS
- existing test files
- Limit for `style.css`: new `.settings-*` rules with tokens only; remove the `.more-jumps`/`.more-jump` rules and the `.more-jump` item in the `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` list; remove the dead `.add-extra-income-form` rule and the `.add-extra-income-form .btn` item of its selector list (keep `.setup-form .btn, .plan-form .btn`); add `#more-profile` and `.settings-group` to the `scroll-margin-top` list. No `:hover` rule (test/cssRules.test.js).
