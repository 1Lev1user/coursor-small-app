## Goal
The README and the user guide stop telling people to convert old Excel files ("Old .xls files must be saved as .xlsx or CSV first.") and describe what the import now does with a Citadele-style statement: .xls files are read, card payments are named after the merchant and dated on the day of the payment, personal codes and account numbers are left out of the entry text, and a category is suggested from the card's merchant category code until a rule exists.
