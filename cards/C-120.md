## Goal
Card payment text often names the place: SEB "ALLE PARAUGS!/OSLO/NOR", Swedbank and Citadele card lines. Today the place is dropped. After this card a row and the stored entry keep an optional `place: { city, country }` when the bank text clearly gives it, so a later card can group a trip (S4.14). Nothing is shown yet except in the entry's bank text, which already holds it.
