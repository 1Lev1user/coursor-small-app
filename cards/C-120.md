## Goal
Card payment text often names the place: SEB "ALLE PARAUGS!/OSLO/NOR", Swedbank and Citadele card lines. Today the place is dropped. After this card a row and the stored entry keep an optional `place: { city, country }` when the bank text clearly gives it, so a later card can group a trip (S4.14). Nothing is shown yet. Today the place is dropped (SEB: city and country) or only partly kept (Citadele: the country code is removed, the city stays in the merchant text). SEB gives city and country; Citadele gives the country only (city empty, because the city is not separated from the merchant name); Swedbank gives none (no documented shape).
